Games
Coming soon

13 ways to turn a run into a game.

We’re building these next. Tap one to see how it plays, then tell us which you want first.

01Territory captureLIVEClub runs claim map zones. Rivals steal them back.

Every run your club logs paints hexes on the Bengaluru map in your colour. Run through a rival’s hex and it flips to you. They get 5 days to take it back before it locks.

ClubsAny runAll week
Open the Turf map
02Checkpoint huntHidden GPS checkpoints. Split up, hit them all first.

The host hides checkpoints along a route. Each one only unlocks when you’re within 20 m of it. Teams split up, cover ground and race to clear every checkpoint before the other side does.

Teams30 to 60 minStrategy
03Capture the flagTwo teams, virtual flags at real landmarks.

Each team’s flag sits at a landmark, like a park gate or a metro station. Reach the other team’s flag, grab it on the map and get it back to your base. Get tagged on the way and the flag drops where you stood.

2 teams45 minSpeed
04Zombie runZombies start 2 min late. Anyone they pass turns.

A few runners start 2 minutes behind as zombies. Anyone they pass is infected and switches sides. Stay human until the finish to win. The app picks the zombies, so nobody knows who’s coming.

EveryoneAny routeChaos
05BountyBeat the club’s fastest on a segment. Take the bounty.

Your club’s fastest runner carries the bounty for the week. Beat their time on the set segment and you collect it, and you become the new target.

ClubOne segmentAll week
06Handicap raceStaggered starts so slow and fast finish together.

The app staggers start times by pace so everyone should cross the line together. The winner is whoever beats their own predicted time by the most. A 7:30 runner can beat a 4:30 runner fair and square.

Club5 to 10 kmFair play
07Prediction runGuess your time. Watch hidden. Closest wins.

Everyone predicts their finish time before the start. Pace and time stay hidden the whole run. Closest guess at the finish wins, not the fastest legs.

EveryoneAny distanceFeel
08Ghost raceRace your past self or last month’s club run.

Pick an old run, yours or the club’s, and it plays back as a ghost on the map in real time. Stay ahead of it. You’ll know the second it passes you.

Solo or clubAny routePB chasing
09Fartlek dropA SPRINT 30s alert hits every phone at once.

Mid-run, the app pushes a SPRINT 30s alert to every phone at the same moment. Whoever covers the most ground in that window takes the point. Nobody knows when the next one drops.

Club runAny routeIntervals
10Relay batonPass a virtual baton across the city.

A virtual baton moves from runner to runner across Bengaluru. Start your run where a clubmate finished theirs and the chain grows. The club with the longest unbroken chain wins the week.

ClubsAll weekTeamwork
11GPS artPlan a route that draws a shape on the map.

The club plans a route that draws a shape, a heart, a dosa, your club logo. Everyone runs their stretch and the drawing fills in live. Built for the screenshot.

ClubPlanned routeShareable
12Run bingoSpot filter coffee, temples and street dogs.

Get a bingo card of things to spot on your route: a filter coffee stall, a temple, a street dog, an auto with a slogan. Snap a photo to mark a square. First full line wins.

EveryoneAny routeEasy pace
13Mystery routeThe route reveals one turn at a time.

Nobody sees the full route. The app shows the next turn only when you reach the last one, so nobody knows where the run ends until you’re there.

ClubSurpriseExploring
